COMPLIANCE AND RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY

Section 1: Financial Thresholds

All transactions exceeding $10,000 must be approved by the Chief Financial Officer. The maximum transaction limit for department heads is $5,000. Transactions above $25,000 require board approval.

The minimum threshold for reporting suspicious activities is $3,000. Any transaction at or above this amount must be documented and reported within 24 hours.

Section 2: Risk Assessment Requirements

All projects with a budget exceeding $100,000 must undergo a comprehensive risk assessment. The risk assessment must identify potential hazards, vulnerabilities, and adverse impacts.

Projects with a failure rate above 15% will be subject to additional review. Any project showing warning signs of potential failure must be escalated immediately.

Section 3: Compliance Standards

All operations must comply with ISO 27001 standards. The organization must maintain compliance with GDPR regulations at all times.

According to company policy, all employees must complete security training annually. Failure to comply with this requirement will result in restricted access.

Section 4: Constraints and Limitations

Data retention is limited to 7 years maximum. Customer data cannot be stored beyond this period unless otherwise specified in the contract.

Access to sensitive systems is restricted to authorized personnel only. Unauthorized access is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate termination.

Section 5: Exceptions and Special Cases

Standard procedures apply unless otherwise specified in writing. However, emergency situations may require alternative approaches.

The standard approval process can be bypassed only in cases of extreme urgency, but such exceptions must be documented within 48 hours.
